Things to notice

  • The strobe dots are evenly spaced horizontally. With no air resistance there is no horizontal force, so vₓ stays constant.
  • At the top of the flight v_y = 0, but the speed is not zero. The ball is still moving at vₓ.
  • The gradient of the v_y–t graph is −g for the whole flight, going up and coming down.
  • From level ground, 30° and 60° give the same range, and 45° gives the maximum. From a height, the best angle is below 45°.
  • Air resistance lowers the range and maximum height, makes the descent steeper than the ascent, and lowers the best angle.
